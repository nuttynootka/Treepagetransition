Put your garland photo in this folder and name it garland.png

- It should be wide (about 3000 x 400 pixels or similar) with a transparent background.
- Until it is here, the header shows a dark green placeholder band.
- To use another name or format, pass garlandSrc="/garland/your-file.webp" to <GarlandHeader>.

See the README section "Garland header" for full steps.
